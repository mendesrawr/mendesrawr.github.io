WA Muster — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

WA Muster is a Chrome extension that exports member lists of WhatsApp groups

and communities that you belong to, from your own WhatsApp Web session.

What WA Muster accesses

  • The names, phone numbers and WhatsApp IDs of members of groups and

    • communities visible in your own WhatsApp Web account, only when you click

    Export, Report, or Snapshot.

  • Your group and community names and member counts, to build the group list.

    • Where the data goes

    Nowhere. WA Muster reads WhatsApp Web locally in your browser. Exports

    (CSV / vCard files) are saved to your own computer. Snapshots used for change

    tracking are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.

    WA Muster has no server, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. The

    developer never receives, sees, or sells your data or your groups' data.

    Payments

    Pro subscriptions are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. When you choose to

    pay, ExtensionPay/Stripe collect your payment details under their own privacy

    policies. WA Muster itself never sees or stores your card details; it only

    receives a yes/no answer to "has this user paid?".

    Permissions, in plain language

  • web.whatsapp.com access — the only website the extension runs on; it is

    • where your groups live.

  • Storage — saves your snapshots, settings and Pro status on your device.
  • Downloads — saves the CSV / vCard files you export.
  • Tabs / Scripting — finds your WhatsApp Web tab and keeps the connection

    • to it working.

    Contact

    Questions: use the support email listed on the WA Muster Chrome Web Store page.
